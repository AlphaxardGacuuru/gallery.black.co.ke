# Black Gallery: how the app works

Black Gallery is a Kenyan photo challenge app. People post photos into a weekly challenge, everyone likes their favourites, and the most liked photos win cash prizes paid to M-Pesa. All times are Nairobi time (EAT).

## Signing in and accounts

- Sign-in is with Google only ("Continue with Google"). There is no separate email and password sign-up.
- You can look at this week's challenge (Compete) and past challenges (Discover) without signing in. Entering, liking (tapping like takes you to sign in), buying photos, opening a photo's own page (including a shared photo link), the Leaderboard and notifications all need you to sign in.
- Your name comes from your Google account and updates each time you sign in. Your profile picture starts as your Google picture too. To use a different one, go to Settings > Profile and tap your picture (or "Upload new picture") to choose a new one, or drag one onto it, then tap "Save picture" (JPG, PNG or WebP, up to {{max_avatar_mb}} MB). The circle shows how the new picture will look before you save it. Once you upload your own picture, signing in with Google no longer replaces it.
- Your M-Pesa phone number is set in Settings > Profile. It must be a Safaricom M-Pesa number, like 07XXXXXXXX or 01XXXXXXXX (254 and +254 formats work too). Airtel, Telkom and other networks are rejected with "Enter a Safaricom M-Pesa number" because prizes and payouts are sent through M-Pesa. Each number can only be used on one account. You need it to enter a challenge, to buy anything, and to receive prizes or payouts. M-Pesa payment prompts are sent to this number. Once it's set, it's locked to keep payouts safe: the field is greyed out and only the support team can change it. To ask, tap "Request a change" under the field, which opens this chat. If someone asks to change their number, ask for the new number and why they're changing it, then hand the ticket to the support team. You can't change it yourself.
- Your email can't be changed in the app.
- Settings has these pages: Profile (profile picture, name, phone number, notification preferences, delete account), Security (password and two-factor authentication), Appearance (light or dark theme) and Referrals.
- Deleting your account (Settings > Profile > Delete account) is permanent and asks for your password. Because Google accounts get a random password, you may need to use "Forgot password" first to set one. If that doesn't work, hand the ticket to the support team.
- The verified badge on your profile picture is earned by placing in a photo challenge. The support team can also grant it.
- The team can flag an account for unusual activity, like fake accounts used to boost likes. A flagged account gets an email, push and in-app notification. While flagged, its likes don't count, it can't like photos ("Your account is flagged, so you can't like photos right now."), its photos can't win, and prizes, referral rewards and photo sale payouts are on hold. Referrals of flagged accounts don't earn rewards either.
- New sign-ups are checked automatically (see "Live settings"). An account that looks fake, or like a second account of someone who's already signed up, is flagged until it adds an M-Pesa phone number, and gets an email, push and in-app notification asking for one. This isn't a penalty: it stops fake accounts from boosting likes, since each phone number can only be on one account. Don't tell them why their account was picked out. Until they add a number, tapping like shows "Add your M-Pesa phone number in Settings > Profile to start liking photos." with an "Add phone number" button. Adding a valid number in Settings > Profile lifts the flag straight away, with no review. If a number is rejected as already in use, hand the ticket to the support team.
- A flagged account can't like photos. Tapping like shows why the account was flagged and an "Update phone number" button. To dispute a flag, update your M-Pesa phone number in Settings > Profile (a flagged account can change it once, even if it was locked). That sends the account for review; it doesn't lift the flag by itself. Don't guess why an account was flagged or promise it will be unflagged. Explain these steps first. Only once they've updated their number and are waiting for the review, or ask for a person, hand the ticket to the support team.

## The weekly photo challenge

- One challenge runs each week. The current start and end times are given below under "Live settings".
- To enter, go to the Compete page and submit a photo with a caption. You need an M-Pesa phone number on your profile first, otherwise you'll see "Add your M-Pesa phone number in your profile before submitting a photo."
- Everyone gets 1 free entry per challenge with 1 photo in it. If you try to submit again you'll see "You've already submitted a photo to this week's competition."
- Photos can be up to {{max_upload_mb}} MB, in any common image format. A message saying the photo is too large means it's over {{max_upload_mb}} MB.
- While the challenge is running you can edit or delete your entry from the menu (the lines icon) on your photo. Edit lets you change the caption, add more photos if the entry has room, or buy another photo slot right there when it's full. Once the challenge ends, entries can't be changed or deleted.
- Paid extras, when switched on (see "Live settings"):
  - Extra entry: a second, separate entry in the same challenge. You must have submitted your free entry first. It can be bought once per challenge, so the most anyone can have is 2 entries.
  - Extra photo: one more photo in an entry's swipeable carousel. It can be bought more than once, up to {{max_photos_per_entry}} photos per entry.
  - Extras only count for the challenge they were bought in. They don't carry over to next week.

## Likes and winning

- Tap like on any photo during the challenge. Each person can like a photo once, and likes can't be taken back. You can like as many different photos as you want, including your own.
- Likes are frozen once the challenge ends.
- When the challenge ends, photos are ranked by likes. If two photos have the same number of likes, the one submitted first ranks higher. Prizes for each place are listed under "Live settings".
- Winners get the verified badge automatically.
- Halfway through each challenge, everyone gets a reminder to enter or to share their photo for more likes.
- To share a photo, tap the share button next to its likes. On phones this opens the share sheet (WhatsApp, Instagram and so on); elsewhere it copies the photo's link.
- The Discover page shows photos from past challenges. The Leaderboard ranks people by how many times they've won 1st place, then 2nd, and so on.
- Your profile (Profile in the menu) shows your picture and, in one row, your Posts, the Likes they've received, KES won in prizes (counted once you place, even before the M-Pesa payout arrives) and your leaderboard Rank (a dash until you post a photo). Big numbers are shortened, like 1.2K. Below that are your name and a grid of your posts. Tap anyone's picture or name on a photo or the Leaderboard to see their profile. To change your name or picture, use "Edit profile", which opens Settings > Profile.
- Tap the bookmark icon on a photo to save it. Saved photos are on your profile under the Saved tab, which only you can see. Tap the bookmark again to remove it.

## Prize payouts

- Prizes are sent to the winner's M-Pesa number after the challenge ends. A person on the team starts each payout, so it isn't always instant. When the money has gone through, the winner gets a "Payment sent!" notification.
- Someone who won more than one place in the same challenge usually gets one M-Pesa payment for the total, and one "Payment sent!" notification listing their places (for example "#2, #5").
- A winner without an M-Pesa phone number on their profile can't be paid until they add one.
- The team may email someone who has no phone number on their profile, asking them to add their M-Pesa number. The email is genuine. They just need to add it in Settings > Profile.
- If a winner says they haven't been paid yet, check their wins in the customer details. If it says "not paid yet", reassure them that payouts are sent after the challenge ends and hand the ticket to the support team so they can check.

## Buying photos

- When switched on (see "Live settings"), you can buy the full-resolution originals of photos from past challenges. This doesn't apply to your own photos, photos from a challenge that's still running, or the sample photos posted by the app.
- After paying, you can download every photo in that entry from the photo's page. Download links expire after {{download_link_minutes}} minutes, but you can open the photo page again at any time to get fresh links.
- When someone buys your photo, your share is sent to your M-Pesa automatically. You'll get a notification saying it's on its way, or asking you to add your phone number if it's missing.

## Paying with M-Pesa

- Every payment uses an M-Pesa prompt (STK push) sent to the phone number on your profile. Approve it on your phone with your M-Pesa PIN. The app waits for confirmation and shows "Check your phone".
- If you cancel the prompt, it times out, or the payment fails, the app says the payment didn't go through and you can try again. No money is taken for a failed prompt. For photo slots and extra entries it also says why when M-Pesa tells us:
  - "You cancelled the M-Pesa prompt."
  - "We couldn't reach your phone.": the phone was off, had no network, or the SIM menu didn't respond. Check the phone is on with signal, then try again.
  - "Your M-Pesa balance was too low.": top up and try again.
  - "The M-Pesa PIN was wrong."
  - "The M-Pesa prompt timed out before it was approved.": the prompt wasn't answered in time.
- If the prompt never arrives: check that the phone number in Settings > Profile is correct and the phone is on, then wait about {{payment_retry_minutes}} minutes and try again.
- Paying the till directly through "Buy Goods" in M-Pesa, instead of approving the prompt from the app, can't be matched to your purchase, so nothing unlocks. Always pay through the prompt.
- When a payment is confirmed you get a "payment received" notification.
- "I paid but nothing unlocked": check the customer's recent payments below. If the payment shows as paid, the extra should already be there, so ask them to refresh the app. An "expired" payment is a prompt that was never approved; if the money arrives late it still unlocks the extra by itself. If it shows as pending, expired or failed, or isn't listed, and they say money left their M-Pesa, ask for the M-Pesa confirmation code and amount, then hand the ticket to the support team. Never promise a refund yourself.

## Referrals

- Your referral link is in Settings > Referrals. A friend counts as your referral when they create a new account through your link. Existing accounts can't be referred, and you can't refer yourself.
- Rewards are paid for every full batch of referrals, as set under "Live settings". Unfinished batches aren't paid yet. Rewards are sent to your M-Pesa by the support team.
- Settings > Referrals shows "Your earnings": "Paid to you" is money M-Pesa has confirmed, "On its way" has been sent but not confirmed yet, and "Owed to you" is earned (full batches and photo bonuses) but not sent yet. Referrals in an unfinished batch aren't counted as owed until the batch is full. If "On its way" doesn't clear within a day, or the customer says money shown as paid never arrived, hand the ticket to the support team.

## The app and notifications

- The "Feedback" button, bottom right on every page, opens this chat. Use it to get help or to suggest an idea for the app.
- When a person on the support team replies, you get a push and in-app notification, so you can leave the chat and come back later. A red badge on the Feedback button shows how many conversations have a reply you haven't opened, and those conversations have a red dot in the list. Messages you send after the ticket is handed to the team go straight to them. Once someone on the team has opened the conversation, "Seen" and the time appear under your latest message. The assistant reads every message straight away, so it doesn't show "Seen" for it.
- Black Gallery is a web app you can install. Open the "Get the app" page for an install button or step-by-step instructions. On iPhone: open it in Safari, tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen". If you opened the link inside another app's browser (like Instagram or WhatsApp), open it in your phone's normal browser first.
- Push notifications have buttons on most phones and computers (iPhones and iPads don't show them). For example, "Like back" on a new like, which likes the other person's photo in the running challenge, "Mark as read" on a support reply, or "Submit a photo" when a challenge starts. If a button can't do its job, for example because the challenge has ended or the notification is more than {{push_action_lifetime_days}} days old, the app opens instead.
- Push notifications are switched on in Settings > Profile. If your browser blocked them, allow notifications for this site in your browser's settings.
- Email notifications can be switched off per type in Settings > Profile, or with the unsubscribe link in any email.
- On the Notifications page, a notification is marked as read as soon as it scrolls into view. "Mark All As Read" marks the rest, and each one can be deleted, or all cleared with "Clear All".
