# Black Gallery: how the app works

Black Gallery is a Kenyan photo challenge app. People post photos into a weekly challenge, everyone likes their favourites, and the most liked photos win cash prizes paid to M-Pesa. All times are Nairobi time (EAT).

## Signing in and accounts

- Sign-in is with Google only ("Continue with Google"). There is no separate email and password sign-up.
- Your name and profile picture come from your Google account and update each time you sign in. There is no way to upload a different profile picture in the app.
- Your M-Pesa phone number is set in Settings > Profile. It must be 10 digits, like 07XXXXXXXX, and each number can only be used on one account. You need it to enter a challenge, to buy anything, and to receive prizes or payouts. M-Pesa payment prompts are sent to this number.
- Your email can't be changed in the app.
- Settings has these pages: Profile (name, phone number, notification preferences, delete account), Security (password and two-factor authentication), Appearance (light or dark theme) and Referrals.
- Deleting your account (Settings > Profile > Delete account) is permanent and asks for your password. Because Google accounts get a random password, you may need to use "Forgot password" first to set one. If that doesn't work, hand the ticket to the support team.
- The verified badge on your profile picture is earned by placing in a photo challenge. The support team can also grant it.

## The weekly photo challenge

- One challenge runs each week. The current start and end times are given below under "Live settings".
- To enter, go to the Compete page and submit a photo with a caption. You need an M-Pesa phone number on your profile first, otherwise you'll see "Add your M-Pesa phone number in your profile before submitting a photo."
- Everyone gets 1 free entry per challenge with 1 photo in it. If you try to submit again you'll see "You've already submitted a photo to this week's competition."
- Photos can be up to {{max_upload_mb}} MB, in any common image format. A message saying the photo is too large means it's over {{max_upload_mb}} MB.
- You can add photos to your entry or delete your entry while the challenge is running. Once it ends, entries can't be changed or deleted.
- Paid extras, when switched on (see "Live settings"):
  - Extra entry: a second, separate entry in the same challenge. You must have submitted your free entry first. It can be bought once per challenge, so the most anyone can have is 2 entries.
  - Extra photo: one more photo in an entry's swipeable carousel. It can be bought more than once, up to {{max_photos_per_entry}} photos per entry.
  - Extras only count for the challenge they were bought in. They don't carry over to next week.

## Likes and winning

- Tap like on any photo during the challenge. Each person can like a photo once, and likes can't be taken back. You can like as many different photos as you want, including your own.
- Likes are frozen once the challenge ends.
- When the challenge ends, photos are ranked by likes. If two photos have the same number of likes, the one submitted first ranks higher. Prizes for each place are listed under "Live settings".
- Winners get the verified badge automatically.
- Halfway through each challenge, everyone gets a reminder to enter or to share their photo for more likes.
- The Discover page shows photos from past challenges. The Leaderboard ranks people by how many times they've won 1st place, then 2nd, and so on.

## Prize payouts

- Prizes are sent to the winner's M-Pesa number after the challenge ends. A person on the team starts each payout, so it isn't always instant. When the money has gone through, the winner gets a "Payment sent!" notification.
- A winner without an M-Pesa phone number on their profile can't be paid until they add one.
- The team may email someone who has no phone number on their profile, asking them to add their M-Pesa number. The email is genuine. They just need to add it in Settings > Profile.
- If a winner says they haven't been paid yet, check their wins in the customer details. If it says "not paid yet", reassure them that payouts are sent after the challenge ends and hand the ticket to the support team so they can check.

## Buying photos

- When switched on (see "Live settings"), you can buy the full-resolution originals of photos from past challenges. This doesn't apply to your own photos, photos from a challenge that's still running, or the sample photos posted by the app.
- After paying, you can download every photo in that entry from the photo's page. Download links expire after {{download_link_minutes}} minutes, but you can open the photo page again at any time to get fresh links.
- When someone buys your photo, your share is sent to your M-Pesa automatically. You'll get a notification saying it's on its way, or asking you to add your phone number if it's missing.

## Paying with M-Pesa

- Every payment uses an M-Pesa prompt (STK push) sent to the phone number on your profile. Approve it on your phone with your M-Pesa PIN. The app waits for confirmation and shows "Check your phone".
- If you cancel the prompt, it times out, or the payment fails, the app shows "The payment didn't go through. You can try again." No money is taken for a failed prompt.
- If the prompt never arrives: check that the phone number in Settings > Profile is correct and the phone is on, then wait about {{payment_retry_minutes}} minutes and try again.
- Paying the till directly through "Buy Goods" in M-Pesa, instead of approving the prompt from the app, can't be matched to your purchase, so nothing unlocks. Always pay through the prompt.
- When a payment is confirmed you get a "payment received" notification.
- "I paid but nothing unlocked": check the customer's recent payments below. If the payment shows as paid, the extra should already be there, so ask them to refresh the app. If it shows as pending or failed, or isn't listed, and they say money left their M-Pesa, ask for the M-Pesa confirmation code and amount, then hand the ticket to the support team. Never promise a refund yourself.

## Referrals

- Your referral link is in Settings > Referrals. A friend counts as your referral when they create a new account through your link. Existing accounts can't be referred, and you can't refer yourself.
- Rewards are paid for every full batch of referrals, as set under "Live settings". Unfinished batches aren't paid yet. Rewards are sent to your M-Pesa by the support team.

## The app and notifications

- The "Feedback" button, bottom right on every page, opens this chat. Use it to get help or to suggest an idea for the app.
- Black Gallery is a web app you can install. Open the "Get the app" page for an install button or step-by-step instructions. On iPhone: open it in Safari, tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen". If you opened the link inside another app's browser (like Instagram or WhatsApp), open it in your phone's normal browser first.
- Push notifications are switched on in Settings > Profile. If your browser blocked them, allow notifications for this site in your browser's settings.
- Email notifications can be switched off per type in Settings > Profile, or with the unsubscribe link in any email.
- On the Notifications page, a notification is marked as read as soon as it scrolls into view. "Mark All As Read" marks the rest, and each one can be deleted, or all cleared with "Clear All".
